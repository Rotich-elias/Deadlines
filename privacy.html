Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 28, 2026

Deadlines&Notes respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when using the app.

Information We Collect

  • Tasks and notes you create.
  • Your Google account information if you choose Google Sign-In.
  • Backup data stored in your Google Drive App Data folder if you enable Google Drive synchronization.
  • Optional synchronization information if you connect your own server.

How Information Is Used

  • Store your tasks and notes.
  • Synchronize data between your devices.
  • Restore your information after reinstalling the app.
  • Provide reminders and notifications.

Data Storage

Your information is stored locally on your device. If you enable Google Drive synchronization, backup data is stored in your Google Drive App Data folder. If you configure your own sync server, your data is sent only to the server you specify.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only transmitted when you choose to enable synchronization.

Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect your information, but no transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

  • You may disable synchronization at any time.
  • You may remove Google account access through your Google Account settings.
  • You may delete local data by uninstalling the application or clearing app data.
