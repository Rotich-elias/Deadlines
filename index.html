Deadlines

Deadlines is an offline-first productivity app that helps you organize tasks, manage notes, schedule reminders, and optionally synchronize your data across devices. Everything works locally on your device, even without an internet connection.

Unlike many task management apps, Deadlines automatically highlights what needs your attention using intelligent color coding and progress indicators, making it easy to see upcoming and overdue work at a glance.

Features

📅 Smart Task Management

Every task is automatically color-coded according to how close its deadline is. Tasks move through blue, green, yellow, amber, orange, and finally red as deadlines approach, helping you instantly identify priorities.

Each task also includes a progress bar that automatically fills as the due date gets closer—no manual tracking required.

📝 Notes Alongside Tasks

Keep notes and tasks together in one place. Create quick notes or detailed write-ups, organize them with tags, and filter them whenever you need.

📱 Works Completely Offline

Deadlines is designed to work entirely offline. No account, login, or internet connection is required to create tasks, write notes, or manage reminders. Your information remains stored locally on your device unless you choose to enable synchronization.

☁️ Optional Synchronization

If you use multiple devices, synchronization can be enabled whenever you choose.

  • Sync using your own Google Drive private App Data folder.
  • Or synchronize with your own self-hosted server.

Deadlines does not operate its own cloud service. Your information is stored only on your device, in your own Google Drive account, or on your own server.

🔔 Smart Reminders

Receive automatic reminders before tasks become due. Reminders are updated whenever you edit a task and are restored automatically after your device restarts.

🏠 Home Screen Widget

View your upcoming deadlines directly from your Android home screen without opening the app.

Privacy

Your privacy comes first.

  • No mandatory account.
  • No subscriptions required.
  • Offline-first design.
  • Optional synchronization only when you choose.
  • Your data always remains under your control.
